How to choose the right traveling box

Small / cabin-style

Best for 1–3 days, carry-on style packing, or as a child-size box. Also useful as a vanity or shoe box on top of a larger suitcase.

Medium

The most common choice for a week of clothes. Easy to roll and usually enough for one person.

Large or full set

Choose a large box or a 3-piece set for family trips, relocation, trading goods or long holidays. Ask our agents which combination is available today.

Care tips so your box lasts

  • Do not sit on the shell or overload it past the zip and latch.
  • Wipe dust with a soft damp cloth. Avoid harsh bleach on coloured shells.
  • Roll on smooth ground when you can; lift over rough gutters so wheels stay true.
  • Keep a tag with your name and the Megep contact numbers inside the box.
  • Store the set nested together in a dry place when you are not traveling.
