What Megep Trading offers

Traveling boxes, not just bags

A traveling box is a hard-sided suitcase with a shell that helps clothes stay neater. Ours include wheels, telescopic handles and mixed sizes so you can travel light or pack for a longer stay.

Who we serve

Students, families, traders, church and school groups, and anyone who needs a reliable box for road, air or interstate travel. We also help people buying gifts or souvenir sets through Megep Prints.

Our promise

Dedicated, consistent and reliable service. Customer satisfaction is our priority. We do not list prices on the website so you can confirm the latest rate and the exact colour available before you pay.

Useful facts before you buy

  • Choose a smaller box for weekend trips and a larger box or full set for long travel.
  • Light colours look bright in photos; darker or printed colours hide scuffs better.
  • A matching small box is useful for shoes, cosmetics or gifts so they do not crush clothes.
  • Tell the agent your destination type (bus, car or flight) so they can recommend a practical size.
